Privacy Policy

Who we are

Reno RD is a marketplace connecting customers with businesses and independent professionals in the Dominican Republic. Privacy contact: donpollolococabarete@gmail.com.

What we collect

Account: email, name, optional phone, language. Requests: your problem description (text, voice or photo), category, city. Chat: messages between customer and professional. Professional verification is performed by our provider Didit (ID document and selfie); Reno RD does not store images of your ID.

How we use it

To match your request with the right professionals (including AI analysis of your description), translate chat automatically, compute reputation (reviews) and keep the platform safe.

Who we share it with

With the professional who answers your request (they see the description and city; your identity is shared when an offer is accepted). With technical providers: Supabase (database), OpenAI (analysis and translation), Didit (identity verification). We never sell your data.

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your account in the app (Settings → Delete account). Transaction records may be retained as dispute evidence where the law allows.

Terms of Use

The service

Reno RD connects customers with businesses and independent professionals. Professionals are NOT Reno RD employees: the service contract is formed directly between customer and professional.

Payments and commission

The customer pays the professional directly. Reno RD charges the professional a 12% commission on jobs completed through the platform.

Rules

Prohibited: false or misleading content, impersonation, using the platform for illegal activities. Accounts that break the rules may be blocked.

Verification

Badges (identity, phone, business) indicate checks performed, not a guarantee of service quality. Choose using reviews and your own judgement.

Liability

Reno RD acts as a technology intermediary and is not liable for the quality of contracted services; we provide dispute tools and good-faith moderation.
